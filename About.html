My hot takes:

  • We have literal super powers. We can fly and lift heavy things and perceive/manipulate events down to attosecond timescales and control matter at sub-atomic levels over planetary scales and heal ourselves from the brink of death and prolong our natural lifespan and... It's not even realistic to write a fictional character with this many super powers.
  • Our strongest super power is our (sometimes abysmally trained and exercised) ability to communicate and collaborate with other humans to achieve common goals. It's a super power that compounds super powers.
  • With these super powers, we have the privilege and responsibility to continue improving the collective conscious experience, such that all consciousness can maximally appreciate this beautiful universe they exist in.
  • We have an immensely exciting decade ahead, when waves of centuries of super power advancements are starting to constructively interfere on an unparalleled scale. We can make more happen per unit time right now than ever before in history; Now is the time to work hard and build something great.
